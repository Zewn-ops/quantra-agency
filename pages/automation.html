frequently asked

questions >_

Will automation replace my staff?

No — and we'd push back if that's the goal. Automation removes the bottlenecks, not the people. The copy-pasting, the double data entry, the admin that eats a morning. Your team gets that time back for the work only people can do.

What tools do you connect?

Generally the ones you already use — your CRM, your inbox, your spreadsheets, your project board. We start by auditing what you've got. Often the fix is connecting three tools you already pay for, not buying a fourth.

Do we have to change all our software?

Almost never. We audit your current stack, keep what works, and cut what you're paying for twice. Ripping everything out is expensive and rarely necessary.

What happens if an automation breaks?

Systems that run quietly still need looking after. Monitoring, fixes and changes roll into an optional flat monthly retainer once the build is live — so when something needs attention, you're not starting a new conversation.

What does it cost?

A fixed, plain-English quote before work starts — no hourly billing, no surprise invoices. The discovery call that scopes it is free and takes 30 minutes.

We don't replace people — we empower them. Invisible systems that cut the manual admin, connect the tools you already use, and give your team their day back.
