how it works

from "help" to "handled"

01

Tell us what's bugging you

Send us a message describing the problem in your own words. Screenshots, voice notes, hand-drawn diagrams on the back of a serviette — all welcome. We'll come back within a day with a few clarifying questions and a sense of how long it'll take.

02

We sort it out

Most things take less than an hour. Bigger jobs we'll quote upfront so there are no surprises. We work in your tools (Excel, Google Sheets, Outlook, whatever you're using) — you don't need to learn anything new.

03

You get a clear handover

We don't just hand back a fixed file and disappear. You get a short written explanation of what we did and how to use it — in plain English, with screenshots if it helps. If you want, we'll record a quick video walkthrough too.

questions you might be

too polite to ask

What counts as a "task"?

A task is one specific thing you want fixed, built, or set up. "Fix my VAT spreadsheet so it calculates correctly" is one task. "Build me three different report templates" is three tasks. If you're not sure, just ask — we'll tell you upfront.

What if my problem is too small to bother with?

Nothing's too small. We'd rather spend 15 minutes fixing the thing that's been irritating you for 6 months than have you struggle with it for another 6. Lite tier exists exactly for this.

Do I need to know what I want before I contact you?

Not at all. Most people start with "this is annoying me, can you take a look?" That's enough. We'll work out what needs doing on the call.

I've been burned by tech people before. How is this different?

Fair question. Three things: we explain everything in plain English, we quote upfront, and you can cancel any time. We also give you a written record of what we did so the next person — even if it's not us — can pick up where we left off.

Do you support Mac, Windows, both?

Both. And ChromeOS, and most things in between. If you can describe what you're working with, we can probably help.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a day or two of our first chat. For urgent things on the Pro tier, often the same day.

What if I just have one thing I need help with — do I have to commit to a retainer?

No. We do once-off work too, billed hourly. The retainers exist because most clients realise after the first task that they want us on speed dial. But there's zero pressure to subscribe.

I'm not sure if my business is "big enough" for this.

If you have a business and you use technology to run it, you're our client. We work with solo professionals all the way up to teams of 30. Nobody's too small.
