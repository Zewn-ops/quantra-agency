frequently asked

questions >_

Do you build custom, or use WordPress and Shopify?

Whichever genuinely fits. If a WordPress or Shopify build gets you there faster and cheaper, we'll say so. What we won't do is force your business into a template's limits and call it a custom site.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. We hand over the site with everything you need to run it, and we'll walk you through it. If you'd rather not touch it, ongoing updates and maintenance are covered by a flat monthly retainer.

Is it mobile-friendly and fast?

That's the point of building custom. Every site is responsive by default and built for speed — slow, broken sites lose leads, which rather defeats the exercise.

Do you handle hosting and maintenance?

We can. Hosting, updates, backups and monitoring roll into an optional retainer after launch. If you'd rather host it yourself, that's fine too — it's your site, on your accounts.

What does a website cost?

You get a fixed, plain-English quote before work starts — no hourly billing. We scope it properly on a free 30-minute call first, so the number you get is the number you pay.
