frequently asked

questions >_

What do I actually get at the end?

Every file format you'll ever need — PNG, SVG, PDF and AI — plus full brand guidelines so anyone you hire later knows exactly how to use them. All of it on your accounts, in your name.

Can you work with our existing brand?

Yes. Not every brand needs tearing down. Sometimes it's a refresh — tightening the logo, fixing the colour system, building the guidelines that never existed. We'll tell you honestly which one you need.

Is it once-off, or ongoing?

Both, and you choose. A once-off identity project stands on its own. If you need a steady stream of graphics — social posts, ads, banners, presentations — the retainer plans turn that into a subscription instead of a scramble. Flat monthly fee, cancel anytime.

What does branding cost?

A fixed quote, agreed before work starts — never hourly. Retainers are a flat monthly fee with no annual lock-in. We'll scope the whole thing on a free call first.
